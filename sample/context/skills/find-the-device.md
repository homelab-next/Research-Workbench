title: Find the device
---
To say which device held an address, look the address up in the lease records: a lease names the device the address was handed to. Look it up in the asset list too, which is complete for internal hosts.

An empty answer from the lease records is only "not seen": the lease records are not complete. Say so in the gaps and limits, and do not cite it as evidence.
