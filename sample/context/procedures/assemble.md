title: Assembly procedure
---
Write one finding at a time. State likelihood and confidence in separate sentences. Name every partial card under gaps.
Never give silence as a reason: an empty result that is not a proven negative over full coverage, or "no … was observed", is a gap. Name it under gaps_and_limits; do not use it to make anything more or less likely in the summary, assessment or alternatives.
